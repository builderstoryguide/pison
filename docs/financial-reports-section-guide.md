# Financial Reports Section Guide

## Overview

The **Financial Reports** section is the centralized location for all Bursar reporting functionality. It provides comprehensive financial analytics, reporting tools, and insights for effective financial management.

## Accessing Financial Reports

### Navigation Path
1. **Sign in as a Bursar**
2. **Navigate to "Financial Reports"** in the sidebar menu
3. **Select the desired report type** from the tabs

### Menu Structure
```
Bursar Portal
├── Dashboard
├── Fee Management
└── Financial Reports ← All reports consolidated here
    ├── Collection Report
    ├── Outstanding Balances
    └── Revenue Trends
```

## Available Reports

### 1. Collection Report
**Purpose**: Monthly fee collection summary with payment method breakdown

**Features**:
- ✅ **Payment Method Analysis** - Breakdown by cash, bank transfer, mobile money
- ✅ **Monthly Trends** - Collection patterns over time
- ✅ **Transaction Details** - Individual payment records
- ✅ **Export Capabilities** - CSV and JSON formats
- ✅ **Filtering Options** - Date range, payment method, student

**Key Metrics**:
- Total transactions
- Total amount collected
- Average payment amount
- Unique students who paid
- Collection efficiency

### 2. Outstanding Balances Report
**Purpose**: Students with outstanding fee balances and overdue payments

**Features**:
- ✅ **Balance Tracking** - Real-time outstanding amounts
- ✅ **Overdue Alerts** - Students past due date
- ✅ **Class-wise Analysis** - Outstanding by class/level
- ✅ **Status Filtering** - Outstanding vs overdue
- ✅ **Export Functionality** - Detailed reports

**Key Metrics**:
- Total outstanding amount
- Number of students with balances
- Overdue students count
- Average outstanding per student
- Days overdue tracking

### 3. Revenue Trends Report
**Purpose**: Revenue trends and analysis by period, class, and fee category

**Features**:
- ✅ **Revenue Analytics** - Monthly/quarterly trends
- ✅ **Class Performance** - Revenue by class
- ✅ **Fee Category Analysis** - Breakdown by fee type
- ✅ **Comparative Analysis** - Period-over-period growth
- ✅ **Top Performers** - Best performing classes/categories

**Key Metrics**:
- Total revenue generated
- Revenue growth rate
- Average revenue per period
- Top revenue-generating classes
- Fee category performance
